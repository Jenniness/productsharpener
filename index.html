How we work together

Seven steps.
Zero ambiguity.

01

Frame and agree

Establish the problem, constraints and decision. Agree responsibilities, outputs and how completion will be assessed.

02

Organise discovery

Plan stakeholder sessions and research. Map the customer or operational journey and confirm access and dependencies.

03

Shape and make

Synthesise insight, recommend a product direction and design or prototype the core experience. Make assumptions and trade-offs explicit.

04

Review and prioritise

Use feedback and available evidence to refine the direction. Build a prioritised roadmap with dependencies and validation milestones.

05

Deliver and hand over

Review agreed outputs, document decisions and learning, and provide a clear recommendation for the next phase.

06

AI where it improves the experience

Define the proposed application and behaviour, make the experience testable, and clarify what technical feasibility work remains.

07

Operating model

I lead the engagement working with your product, engineering, data and risk specialists. Roles and responsibilities made explicit from day one.
